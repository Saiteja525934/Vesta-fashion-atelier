The Atelier Journal

Pure Geometry Meets Natural Tactility

Founded in 2018 between Milan and Stockholm, VESTA represents a convergence of classical Italian drapery and Nordic minimalism. Every pattern is drawn by hand, testing how fabric moves with the human body in motion.

We source directly from family-owned heritage spinning mills in Biella, Italy, ensuring 100% fiber traceability from pasture to garment bag. No synthetic blends, no microplastics, and zero compromised seams.
